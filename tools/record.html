🎙️ Qalam Studio

Record a real voice for any sound in the game. Tap Record, say it clearly, tap Stop, listen, then Save. Saved recordings replace the computer voice straight away.

Tips: use a quiet room, hold the phone or laptop about a hand-span away, and say each sound once, clearly and slowly — the way you would to a child. Letter names are said as names (Jeem = جِيم); syllables are just the sound (جَ = “ja”).
